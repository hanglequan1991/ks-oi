## Steps
1. Open `/en/bill`.
2. Read `<html lang>`, title, intro, six column headers, button texts, the grand total label, the status area, the note label, the fee note and the disclaimer.
3. Switch back to `/bill` and compare the same elements.

## Expected results
1. `<html lang="en">`; the title, columns (`#`, Hotel, Room, Night, Pricing, Total), buttons (Add row, Clear all, Print / Save PDF, Clear draft), grand total label and the note label (exactly `NOTE`) are English; no Vietnamese label text remains.
2. The fee note reads `Includes 10% service fee` and the disclaimer is the English seeded text from the settings (exactly once on the page, inside the bill notes block).
3. `/bill` shows the Vietnamese equivalents (`GHI CHÚ` for the note label), so the two pages differ only in language.

## Test data
- Seeded default settings (TC-4): fee 10, fee note on, Vietnamese and English disclaimer texts. Read the expected strings from the harness settings object, do not hard-code the disclaimer.
