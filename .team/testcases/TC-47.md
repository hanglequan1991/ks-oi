## Steps
1. On `/bill` pick `Mộng Hotel` then `Deluxe` in row 1.
2. Read Pricing, Night and Total; read the whole page text and `localStorage`.
3. In row 2 pick `Mộng Hotel` then `Standard`.
4. In row 3 pick `Sao Hà Nội` / `Superior`; repeat on `/en/bill` for row 1.

## Expected results
1. Row 1 Pricing shows `1.375.000` (vi) / `1,375,000` (en) and Total `1.375.000` for Night 1. The base price `1.250.000` / `1250000` appears nowhere in the page text, inputs or localStorage.
2. Row 2 (`Standard`, no price): Pricing is empty and Total shows a dash (`—`), and no `edited` marker.
3. Row 3 shows `1.078.000`.
4. Prices come with the room selection without a page reload (wait for the `/api/hotels/mong-hotel` response).
