## Preconditions
- Harness (built by qa under TC-40, reused here): `sheet-rag/e2e/support/billServer.ts` exports `startBillServer()` returning `{ url, requests, setFee(percent), setSettings(partial), close() }`. No Cloudflare credentials are needed.
- TASK-26..TASK-39 are done (page, bundle, styles, routes, Playwright tooling). The test file starts its own server on a free port in `beforeAll`, closes it in `afterAll`, and uses a fresh browser context per test (empty localStorage). Browser: Chromium (`npx playwright install chromium`).

## Steps
1. In Night type `abc`, `-5`, `2.5`, `12a3`, then `0`, `366`, `999999`, `365` and clear the field then blur.
2. In Pricing type `abc`, `1500000`, `1500000000000` (13 digits), `12345678901` and `1.5`; paste `1,250,000 VND`.
3. On `/en/bill` repeat the Pricing entries `1500000` and `abcd`.
4. Move the caret into the middle of a price and type one more digit.

## Expected results
1. Night keeps only digits; letters, minus and decimal point are dropped; `0` becomes or stays 1 (no value below 1), `366` and `999999` are limited to 365 (or rejected back to the previous valid value), and an empty Night returns to `1` on blur.
2. Pricing `1500000` shows `1.500.000` (vi) and `1,500,000` (en); letters are ignored; entries over 10 digits are cut at 10 digits (`1.500.000.000` = 1500000000 for the 13-digit input, `1.234.567.890` for the 11-digit one).
3. The paste yields `1.250.000`; the caret stays where the user typed (the new digit lands in the middle, not at the end).

## Test data
- Sample data (TASK-16, final prices at the seeded 10 percent fee): `Mộng Hotel` (`mong-hotel`): room `Deluxe` 1,375,000 (base 1,250,000 must never appear), room `Standard` no price. `Sao Hà Nội` (`sao-hotel`): room `Superior` 1,078,000. `Đông Đô` (`dong-do-hotel`): no rooms. `Mộng Review` and `Mộng Hidden` are not published and must never be offered.
