## Steps
1. Pick `Mộng Hotel` / `Deluxe` (default 1.375.000).
2. Confirm there is no marker, then change Pricing to `1.200.000`.
3. Click `use default price` (reset).
4. Edit the price again to `900.000`, then change the room to `Standard`; then change the hotel to `Sao Hà Nội` and room `Superior`.
5. Type a price in a row with a free-text hotel (no default).

## Expected results
1. A fresh default price shows no `edited` marker and no reset action.
2. After typing a different price the row shows the `edited` marker and the reset action, and Total follows the new price.
3. Reset puts back `1.375.000`, the marker and the reset action disappear, and Total is 1.375.000.
4. After picking another room (and another hotel) the edited price `900.000` is kept, the marker remains while the new room has a default price.
5. A typed price in a row with nothing to reset to shows no marker and no reset action.
