## Steps
1. Row 1: `Mộng Hotel` / `Deluxe`, Night 2 -> read Total and grand total.
2. Add row 2: `Sao Hà Nội` / `Superior`, Night 3.
3. Add row 3: `Mộng Hotel` / `Standard` (no price), Night 5.
4. Change row 2 price to `1.000.000`, then Night of row 1 to 4.
5. Remove row 2. Repeat the first values on `/en/bill`.

## Expected results
1. Row 1 Total `2.750.000`; grand total `2.750.000` (updates immediately after each keystroke, no button).
2. Row 2 Total `3.234.000`, grand total `5.984.000`; row 3 shows a dash and counts as 0.
3. After the edits: row 2 Total `3.000.000`, row 1 Total `5.500.000`, grand total `8.500.000`.
4. After removing row 2 the grand total is `5.500.000`.
5. English page uses `,` separators with the same numbers.
