## Steps
1. Focus the Hotel field of row 1 with Tab; type `mo`; press ArrowDown twice and ArrowUp once; press Enter.
2. Tab to Room, press ArrowDown to open the list, move with arrows, press Escape, reopen, pick `Deluxe` with Enter.
3. In the Hotel field open a list and press Escape; press Tab with the list open.
4. Read `aria-expanded`, `aria-activedescendant`, `role` values during the steps.

## Expected results
1. Arrow keys move the highlighted option (`aria-selected="true"`, `aria-activedescendant` updates) and wrap or stop at the ends without errors; Enter picks the highlighted option and closes the list.
2. Escape closes the list without changing the typed text; Tab closes the list and moves focus on while keeping the typed text.
3. The fields expose `role=combobox` with `aria-expanded` reflecting the list state; the list has `role=listbox`; the whole flow (hotel, room, night, price, note, add) works with no mouse event.
