## Preconditions
- Needs the deployed Worker with the real D1 database loaded (STORY-14 data) or the owner's sample data; skipped when `LIVE_BASE_URL` is not set. Read-only: the test only opens pages and GETs; it does not write anything. Optional `LIVE_BASE_PRICE_STRINGS` (base prices known to the owner) for the base price check. Keep below 40 requests.

## Steps
1. Open `LIVE_BASE_URL/bill` and `/en/bill`; read headers, meta, disclaimer, fee note.
2. Pick the first hotel offered by typing the first letter(s) of a hotel name taken from `/api/hotels?limit=1`, then its first priced room.
3. Compare the Pricing value with the final price from `/api/hotels/<slug>` and search the page for `LIVE_BASE_PRICE_STRINGS`.
4. Collect every `a[href]` on `/`, `/en/`, a hotel page and the search page; request `/sitemap.xml` and `/robots.txt`.

## Expected results
1. Both pages answer 200 without sign-in in the right language, with `X-Robots-Tag: noindex, nofollow` and the noindex meta tag.
2. The disclaimer and the fee note come from the real settings; no phone, call button or Zalo link.
3. Pricing equals the API's final price (with the real fee), and no base price string appears in the page.
4. No page links to `/bill` or `/en/bill`; sitemap and robots.txt contain no `bill`; no admin function is reachable from the page.

## Test data
- The owner's real data; no fixed hotel names assumed.
